A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you
make your move.

Answers to a few questions you might have.

How much does moving cost?

Pricing depends on the distance, the quantity and size of your items, and the services you need. Contact us for a quote based on your move.

Do you handle fragile items?

Yes. We take extra care when packing and handling delicate belongings. Let us know about fragile items when requesting your quote.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp to discuss your move. We will arrange an inspection if needed, send a quotation, and help you confirm your moving date.
